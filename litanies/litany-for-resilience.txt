Litany for Resilience

I have been burned, and I am still here.
I am the seed in the ashes.
The fire took what it could; it could not take the root.
Failure is not my end. It is the ground I grow from.
I breathe in, and my breath is strong.
I breathe out, and the sting of defeat goes with it.
What has fallen, I will rebuild. What I have lost, I will regain.
The scorched earth is rich, and I am already growing.
I bend, but I do not break. I fall, but I rise.
I am resilient. I am healing. I am thriving.
When the season turns, green will cover the black.
From the ashes, I rise stronger.


Short version

I have been burned, and I am still here.

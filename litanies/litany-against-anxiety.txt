Litany Against Anxiety

I am unshakeable.
I am the lighthouse on the rock.
Ships pass by and have their opinions; the light does not dim for any of them.
I do not shine to be approved of. I shine because I am here.
I draw breath, and I stand taller.
I release it, and the need to please goes with it.
What people think of me is only the water at my feet. It breaks, and it falls away.
I speak, and I do not apologise. I stand, and I do not flinch.
If a ship turns away, my light is still whole.
I am enough. I am steady. I am here.
When the night has passed, the waves will have forgotten, and the rock will still be standing.
From the rock, I shine as myself.

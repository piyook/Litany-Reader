Litany Against Anxiety

I am unshakeable.
I do not answer to their eyes.
Let them stare. Let them judge. I will not shrink.
Their opinion holds no authority over me. I owe their approval nothing.
I draw breath, and I stand taller.
I release it, and the need to please goes with it.
What they think of me is not mine to carry. I leave it where it falls.
I speak, and I do not apologise. I stand, and I do not flinch.
If they turn away, I am still whole.
I am enough. I am strong. I am here.
When the moment has passed, they will have forgotten, and I will still be standing.
From strength, I live as myself.

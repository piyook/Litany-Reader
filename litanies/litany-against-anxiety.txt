Litany Against Anxiety

My heart is racing, but I am safe.
This is an alarm, not a fire.
My body rings the bell to protect me.
I thank it, and I check the house.
There is no fire, only a false alarm.
I draw breath, slow and low, into my belly.
I release it, and my heart begins to slow.
I feel my feet on the floor. I feel the weight of my hands.
I name what I can see, and I am back in this room.
I do not have to fight the feeling. I let it pass through me.
The ringing rises, and then it fades. It always fades.
I am safe. I am here. I am settling.
When the bell falls quiet, I will still be standing.

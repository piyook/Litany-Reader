Litany Against Anxiety

My heart is racing, but I am safe.
This is an alarm, not a fire.
My body rings the bell to protect me.
I thank it, and I check the house.
There is no fire.
This is only a false alarm.
I breathe in, slow and low, into my belly.
I slowly breathe out, and my heart begins to slow.
I feel my feet on the floor.
I feel the weight of my hands.
I name what I can see, and I am back in this room.
I do not have to fight this feeling.
I let it pass through me.
The ringing rises, and then it fades as it always does.
I am safe. I am here. I am centred.

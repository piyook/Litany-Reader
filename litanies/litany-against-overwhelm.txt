Litany Against Overwhelm

I do not need to cross the whole river.
I need only the next stone.
The far bank is not today's work.
The water is wide, and the stone beneath my foot is solid.
I breathe in, and I look down at one stone.
I breathe out, and my mind falls quiet.
Everything cannot be done at once. One thing can be done now.
I step, and I find my footing. I step again.
What I cannot manage, I do not carry. What waits, can wait.
I am capable. I am steady. I am here.
When the river is behind me, I will not remember the width, only the steps.
From one stone, I cross.


Short version

Litany of Gratitude

I have enough.
I am the table set in morning light.
Look at what is already here: breath, warmth, a roof, a hand to hold.
I do not wait for more to be glad.
I breathe in, and I give thanks for it.
I breathe out, and the hunger for what is missing goes with it.
Small things are not small. A cup of tea. A kind word. A quiet hour.
I notice, and noticing is a kind of wealth.
What I have been given, I receive. What I can give, I offer.
I am fortunate. I am full. I am here.
When the day has ended, the light will have been enough.
From gratitude, I live richly.

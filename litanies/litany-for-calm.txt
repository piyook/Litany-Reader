Litany for Calm

I am calm.
I am the centre of the storm.
Winds rage around me, but within is quiet.
Noise is not my master. Haste is not my lord.
I draw breath, and the breath is slow.
I release it, and all tension leaves.
Feelings beat against me like rain against glass.
I notice them, and let them fall.
I watch the storm, but I am not the storm.
The storm turns, but I do not turn with it.
I am rooted. I am steady. I am here.
When the storm has passed, the stillness remains.
From stillness, I act with calm, clear intent.

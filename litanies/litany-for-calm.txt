Litany for Calm

I am calm.
I am the centre of the storm.
Around me winds rage; within me, there is only quiet.
Noise is not my master. Haste is not my lord.
I draw breath, and the breath is slow.
I release it, and all tension goes with it.
What pulls at me, I notice and let drift by, like rain against glass.
I watch the storm, but I am not the storm.
The storm turns, but I do not turn with it.
I am rooted. I am steady. I am here.
When the storm has passed, the stillness will remain.
From stillness, I act with clear, logical intent.

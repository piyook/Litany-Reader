Litany for Sleep

The day is done, and I set it down.
I am the tide, going slowly out.
What is unfinished will keep until morning.
There is nothing left to solve tonight.
I breathe in, and my breath is long.
I breathe out, and my body grows heavy.
My shoulders loosen. My jaw softens. My hands lie open.
Thoughts drift past like boats on dark water, and I do not follow them.
The night holds me. The dark is kind.
I am safe. I am still. I am sinking into rest.
When the morning comes, I will meet it renewed.
Now, I sleep.

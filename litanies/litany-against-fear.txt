Litany Against Fear

I shall not fear fear.
Fear is a shadow, shaped by everything I have not seen or understood.
It falls across me: heart loud, hands unsteady, voice breaking.
These are only the body jumping at its own shadow.
If I run, it lengthens behind me.
If I turn toward the lamp, it shortens at my feet.
So I turn. I hold the light steady.
I learn its shape. What I know can no longer rule me.
The shadow has no body and no power but the power I lend it.
I take that power back.
The shadow was never the room.

Short version

I shall not fear fear:
a shadow cast by the unknown.
I turn. Light arrives.

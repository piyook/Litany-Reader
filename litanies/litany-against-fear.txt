Litany Against Fear

Fear does not rule me.
I accept my fear and breathe slowly.
Fear is a shadow, cast by things unknown.
If I run, it lengthens behind me.
If I raise a lamp, it shortens at my feet.
So I turn to face it, holding my light steady.
I learn what’s in the dark.
The shadow has no power but the power I lend it.
I take that power back.
The shadow was never the room.

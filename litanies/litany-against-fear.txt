Litany Against Fear

I feel fear, but I will not let it stop me.
My heart pounds, my hands shake, and my voice breaks, but these are only signs.
Fear is a monster with no face, made of everything I do not know.
If I run from it, I grow weaker. If I face it, I grow stronger.
So I let go, and I turn toward it.
I force myself to look until I know it, and what I know can no longer rule me.
The monster has no face and no power.
I can see I had nothing to be afraid of after all.

Short version

I feel fear, but I will not let it stop me.
Running makes me weaker. Facing it makes me stronger.

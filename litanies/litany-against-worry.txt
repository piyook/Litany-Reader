Litany Against Worry

I will not live tomorrow today.
I am the open sky; worries are like birds.
I see them come.
To worry is human. I do not fight it.
I name it, and it loosens its hold.
I draw a circle around what I can control.
Inside it, I act. Outside it, I let go.
Most of what I dread will never land.
I breathe, and turn to what is here: this task, this small good thing.
More birds will come and I will watch them pass.
From now on, I live only today.


Short version

I will not live tomorrow today.

Litany Against Worry

I will not live tomorrow today.
I am the open sky, and worries are like birds.
They may cross me; I need not let them stay.
Worry borrows trouble from a day not yet here, and pays for it with this one.
I draw breath, and I am in this hour.
I release it, and the what-ifs fly away.
Most of what I dread will never land. What does I will deal with then.
What I can do, I do now. What I cannot, I leave to the wind.
Tomorrow will bring its own strength, as today brought mine.
I am present. I am open. I am strong.
When the birds have flown, the sky will still be clear.
From this hour, I live today.

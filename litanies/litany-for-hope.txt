Litany for Hope

However dark this hour, it's not my last.
I am walking a long road that bends toward light.
Good things are coming, and I will be ready.
Somewhere ahead is laughter, rest, and love.
I draw breath, and focus on better days to come.
I release it, and I let go of today's troubles.
There are bright mornings ahead.
There are people who I will love.
The ground beneath me is hard now, but it is not where I will stay.
What is broken will mend. 
What is lost will be found.
I am heading toward something great, and it is heading toward me.
With hope, I move forward.

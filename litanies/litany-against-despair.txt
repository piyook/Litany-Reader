Litany Against Despair

I shall not despair.
Despair is the thief of tomorrow.
Despair is the fog that says there is no road, while the road lies beneath my feet.
I will not obey it. I will not argue with it. I will name it, and I will walk on.
I draw breath, and I am still here.
I release it, and the weight leaves a little with it.
What feels endless is only a night, and every night has to run its course.
I need not see the whole path. I need only take the next step.
When the dark presses in, I will light one small flame and keep it burning.
I am bruised, but I am not broken. I am tired, but I am not finished. I am here.
When the fog has lifted, the road will still be there.
From the ashes of despair, I rise.

Litany for Patience

I am patient.
I am the river that carves the stone.
I do not need to win today; I need only to keep flowing.
Time is not my enemy. Time is my ally.
I draw breath, and I am in no hurry.
I release it, and the urge to act hastily leaves.
I add each small effort and let it gather, like drops filling the sea.
I see the distance, but I am not daunted by it.
The seasons turn, and I turn with them, steady.
I am persistent. I am unhurried. I am here.
When the river has run its course, the canyon will remain.
From patience, I will build a lasting victory.

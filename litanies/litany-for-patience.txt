Litany for Patience

I am the river that carves through stone.
I am the wind that turns mountains to dust.
I am patient. I am unhurried. I am inevitable.
Time is not my enemy, time is my ally.
I breathe in, and I do not rush.
I breathe out, and the urge to react goes.
The seasons turn, and I turn with them, steady.
When the river has run its course, the canyon will remain.
I do not need to win today, I only need to wait.
From patience I will build a lasting victory.

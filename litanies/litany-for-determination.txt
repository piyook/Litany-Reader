Litany for Determination

I have chosen my road, and I will follow it to the end.
The hill is steep, but my legs are steady.
Doubt may speak, but it does not decide.
I draw breath, and I move forward.
I release it, and the urge to turn back leaves.
I may feel pain and hardship, but this is temporary.
What is difficult, I will do slowly. What is slow, I will finish.
Every step I take is a step nearer my goal.
I am determined. I am steady. I am moving.
One step, and then the next, until I arrive at the top.
When I look back, the hill will be behind me.

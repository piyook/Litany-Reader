Litany Against Regret

The past is the tide going out.
I am the shore, and I let it go.
What is done is written in the sand, and the sea has already softened it.
I cannot walk back to change a single step.
I breathe in, and I am here.
I breathe out, and the weight of what-if goes with it.
What I did, I did with what I knew then.
What I learned, I carry. What I regret, I lay down.
The footprints behind me are not a chain; they are only proof that I have walked.
I look ahead, where the sand is smooth and the path is open.
When the tide has gone, the shore will still be mine.
From here, I take the next step.

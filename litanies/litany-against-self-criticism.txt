Litany Against Self-Criticism

I am mended, not broken.
I am the bowl that cracked and was made whole with gold.
The cracks are not my shame; they are where the light comes through.
Harsh words are not truth, though they speak in my own voice.
I draw breath, and I soften.
I release it, and the need to be perfect goes with it.
I speak to myself as I would to a friend I love.
I have made mistakes; I have not been made worthless by them.
What I have broken, I can mend. What I have learned, I keep.
I am worthy. I am whole. I am enough.
